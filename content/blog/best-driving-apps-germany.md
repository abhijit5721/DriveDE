The honest answer first: **there is no single best German driving app, because getting the licence is two completely different problems.** The theory exam is a flashcard problem. The practical training is a skills-and-money problem, at €70 to €95 per lesson. Nearly every well-known app only solves the first one.

## Category 1: theory apps (a crowded, fine market)

These train the **official TÜV/DEKRA question catalogue**, the exact pool your theory exam questions come from:

- **Fahren Lernen Max**: usually included via your driving school's learning package, syncs your progress with your instructor.
- **ADAC Führerschein app**: a solid free way into the catalogue.
- **Theorie24, iTheorie and similar**: standalone catalogue trainers, one-time or subscription (check current pricing).

You cannot go far wrong here: serious providers all license the same official catalogue, and most offer English among the exam languages. Usually the sensible choice is simply whatever your driving school already includes.

## Category 2: the practical part, where the map goes empty

From your first driving lesson the problem changes completely. Now the questions are: how many €80 lessons do you still need? Which mistakes keep repeating? Are you objectively exam-ready, or does it just feel that way? A question-catalogue trainer has no answer to any of that.

## The honest recommendation

Realistically you want **both**: a theory trainer for the theory exam (often already included through your school) and a practical companion for the expensive part. A failed theory exam costs about €100 to repeat; a [failed practical exam costs €600 or more](/blog/failed-german-driving-test/), and practice lessons are by far the [biggest cost block](/blog/german-driving-licence-cost/). The practical side is where an app can actually save you money.
