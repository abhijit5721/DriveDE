Good news first: the United States is on Germany's **Anlage 11 FeV** list. That means a US driver's license from **any of the 50 states or Washington D.C.** can be exchanged for a German licence **without taking the theory or practical exam**. The process is administrative: paperwork, not tests.

## The 6-month rule

After you register your residence in Germany (Anmeldung), you may keep driving on your US license for **6 months**. After that, driving without a German licence counts as driving without a licence, which is a criminal offence, not a parking-ticket kind of problem. Start the exchange early: appointment slots at the Führerscheinstelle can take weeks, and processing another 3 to 8 weeks.

## Step by step

1. **Book an appointment** at your local Führerscheinstelle (driving licence office). In big cities, do this the week you arrive.
2. **Get a certified translation** of your US license. Most offices accept translations from ADAC or a sworn translator (usually €30 to €80). Some offices waive this for English-language licences: ask yours first.
3. **Gather your documents**: passport, Anmeldebestätigung (residence registration), a biometric photo, your US license, the translation, and proof of when the license was issued (a driving record from your state DMV helps if the issue date is not printed on the card).
4. **Submit and pay** the application fee, typically €35 to €45.
5. **Hand in your US license** when you collect the German one. Germany usually keeps it or invalidates it; some offices return it to the issuing state.

## What you do NOT need

- No theory exam
- No practical exam
- No driving school
- Usually no eye test or first-aid course (a few offices ask for an eye test; it costs about €7 at any optician)

## Watch out for these

- **The license must have been issued before you registered German residence.** A license obtained during a quick trip home after moving will not be exchanged.
- **Automatic-only is not a restriction from the US side.** Your German licence will be issued for manual and automatic alike, because the exchange does not re-examine you.
- **Renewals and duplicates** can confuse offices if the original issue date is missing. Order your state driving record before the appointment.

## Cost and timeline overview

| Item | Typical cost |
|---|---|
| Application fee | €35-45 |
| Certified translation | €30-80 (sometimes waived) |
| Biometric photo | €10 |
| Total | **€75-135** |

Processing takes 3 to 8 weeks depending on the office. You keep driving on your US license in the meantime, as long as you are within the 6-month window.

## Planning to drive a lot in Germany?

Even though you skip the exams, German traffic rules differ in ways that surprise US drivers: right-before-left priority in residential streets, mandatory Rettungsgasse on the Autobahn, and speed cameras everywhere. A few practice drives with feedback go a long way.
