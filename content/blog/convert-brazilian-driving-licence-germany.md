Brazil is **not** on Germany's Anlage 11 FeV list. Converting a CNH (Carteira Nacional de Habilitação) therefore requires passing the **German theory exam and practical exam**. The compensation: converters have **no mandatory lesson hours**. The 12 compulsory Sonderfahrten that make a first German licence so expensive simply do not apply to you.

## The 6-month rule

After registering residence in Germany, your CNH (together with a translation or an international permit) is valid for **6 months** of driving. The conversion realistically takes 3 to 6 months including exams, so apply in your first weeks, not month five.

## Step by step

1. **Apply at the Führerscheinstelle** with: passport, Anmeldebestätigung, biometric photo, your CNH plus a **certified translation** (ADAC or a sworn translator; consulate translations are accepted by some offices), an **eye test** (about €7) and a **first-aid course certificate** (9 units, €40 to €60).
2. **Register with a driving school**: formally required to book the exams through TÜV or DEKRA. Say it is an Umschreibung and ask for an assessment drive first; a fair school will estimate your real lesson need after one or two drives.
3. **Theory exam**: 30 questions from the official catalogue. The exam is offered in **Portuguese** as well as English and German. The format differs from the DETRAN test: scenario questions where several answers can be correct at once.
4. **Practical exam**: about 55 minutes with an official examiner, conducted in German. Learn the standard commands ahead of time.

## What catches Brazilian drivers

- **Right-before-left**: at unmarked residential junctions the car from the right goes first. It is tested on nearly every exam route.
- **Absolute lane discipline**: undertaking on the Autobahn fails you instantly; keep right except when overtaking.
- **Procedure visibility**: examiners score the visible routine (mirror, shoulder check, signal) on every single maneuver, not just correct outcomes.
- **Parking under observation**: rückwärts einparken (reverse parking) on command is part of the exam; practice it until it is boring.

## Realistic budget

| Item | Typical cost |
|---|---|
| Application, translation, eye test, first aid | €120-180 |
| Theory exam fee + materials | €60-110 |
| Driving school admin fee | €100-250 |
| Driving lessons (typical converter: 5-15) | €350-1,400 |
| Practical exam fee | €230-320 |
| Total | **€850-2,250** |

Your years behind the wheel are an asset, but only disciplined preparation converts them into a first-attempt pass. Every failed practical costs €600 or more in re-test fees and mandatory extra lessons, so the cheapest strategy is to go in objectively ready.

## One project, two skills

Passing in Germany means two separate things: knowing the German rules (theory) and demonstrating the German procedure (practical). Treat them as separate workstreams with their own practice plans, and track both instead of guessing.
