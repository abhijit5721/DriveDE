# Directory submissions pack, 16 September 2026

Purpose: backlinks and domain rating for the blog, not signups. Product Hunt and its clones reach founders and tech people, not German learner drivers, so the expected outcome is 20 to 30 do-follow or brand links over one afternoon and a domain-rating lift over the following two months. Treat it as a one-off SEO job. The Product Hunt launch itself is parked until the funnel numbers and the share card are in; launch once, on a Tuesday, with the no-account trainer as the demo.

Rules:
- Submit from the founder's own accounts (the founder types every password; Claude Code prepares the text and can pre-fill forms in the automation Chrome on request).
- Always the canonical URL https://www.drivede.app/ with no UTM in directory profile fields (many directories strip or nofollow parameterised links); use UTM only where a directory has a separate "website" and "launch link" field, and then only on the launch link: `?utm_source=<directory>&utm_medium=directory&utm_campaign=listing-2026-09`.
- German directories get the German copy, everything else the English copy.
- No claims we cannot back: no "free app" (the trainer is free, the app is a seven-day trial then a pass), no user numbers, no "AI-powered".

Short description (160 chars, EN): Interactive trainers for the German practical driving test: right before left, roundabouts, parking, exam commands. Try the first two in the browser, no account.

Short description DE: Interaktive Trainer für die praktische Fahrprüfung: Rechts vor links, Kreisverkehr, Einparken, Prüferansagen. Die ersten zwei direkt im Browser, ohne Konto.

Long description (EN):
DriveDE is a companion app for people preparing for the German driving licence, in German and English. Driving lessons in Germany cost 70 to 115 euros an hour and one in three candidates fails the practical exam. DriveDE lets you rehearse the situations that fail people most often before you sit in the car: right before left at unmarked junctions, roundabout entry and exit, multi-lane turns, parallel and bay parking, and the examiner's commands on the exam route. Every trainer is a hand-built interactive scene with exact geometry, not a video and not a quiz. The right-before-left and roundabout trainers run in the browser without an account. The full curriculum, the readiness gauge, exam countdown and licence-conversion guides come with a seven-day free trial, then a one-off pass. Built in Hamburg by a founder who went through the process as an expat.

Long description (DE):
DriveDE ist die Begleit-App für die deutsche Fahrausbildung, auf Deutsch und Englisch. Eine Fahrstunde kostet 70 bis 115 Euro, und jeder Dritte fällt durch die praktische Prüfung. Mit DriveDE übst du die Situationen, an denen die meisten scheitern, bevor du im Auto sitzt: Rechts vor links an Kreuzungen ohne Schilder, Ein- und Ausfahren im Kreisverkehr, mehrspuriges Abbiegen, Längs- und Querparken und die Ansagen des Prüfers auf der Prüfungsstrecke. Jeder Trainer ist eine von Hand gebaute interaktive Szene mit exakter Geometrie, kein Video und kein Quiz. Rechts vor links und Kreisverkehr laufen direkt im Browser ohne Konto. Der komplette Lehrplan, die Prüfungsreife-Anzeige, der Prüfungs-Countdown und die Umschreibungs-Guides gibt es sieben Tage kostenlos zum Testen, danach als einmaligen Pass. Gebaut in Hamburg.
