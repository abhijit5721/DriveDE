For everyone who moved here with a foreign driving licence: whether you can simply swap it or have to take the German tests depends on the country it was issued in (the "Anlage 11" list). I am going through the German practical test myself in Hamburg and wrote the whole thing down in one place, country by country: what you can convert, what the tests cost, and the situations experienced drivers from abroad usually get caught on, like right before left at unmarked junctions and roundabouts that run the other way round.

There is also a free page per exam situation that explains what the examiner wants to see, with a two-minute trainer, no account needed: https://www.drivede.app/exam/?utm_source=facebook&utm_campaign=munich-expats
