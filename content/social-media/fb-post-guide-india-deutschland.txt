For everyone from India converting their licence here: India is not on Germany's "Anlage 11" list, so you have to pass both the German theory and the practical exam. The good news is that there are no mandatory lesson hours, so how much you spend depends mostly on how prepared you are.

I am doing my practical test in Hamburg myself and wrote the whole process down in one place: documents, costs, and the things experienced Indian drivers usually get caught on here, like right-before-left at unmarked junctions and roundabouts that run the other way round.

The guide: https://www.drivede.app/blog/convert-indian-driving-licence-germany/?utm_source=facebook&utm_campaign=indians-in-deutschland

At the end there is a free two-minute right-of-way trainer if you want to test yourself, no account needed. Happy to answer questions in the comments.
