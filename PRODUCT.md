# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Learner drivers in Germany, two segments: (1) English-speaking expats converting or acquiring a license (Umschreibung; often anxious about bureaucracy and language), (2) cost-conscious German students aged 17-26 trying to cut down on 75-95 EUR driving lessons. Context of use: on the phone, between or during driving lessons, sometimes mounted in a car. Job to be done: know objectively when they are ready to book the Fahrpruefung, and make every paid lesson count.

## Product Purpose

DriveDE is a practical-driving companion app: GPS lesson tracking with a one-tap mistake log, an objective exam-readiness score, interactive trainers (cockpit/shifting, pre-drive check, maneuvers in 3D), a structured curriculum for every Sonderfahrt, and an Umschreibung guide per country. It exists because every competitor is theory-quiz-only and readiness is otherwise decided by the instructor who sells the lessons. Success: users pass on the first attempt and credit the app for booking at the right time.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, precise, encouraging. Voice: direct, bilingual (DE/EN), concrete numbers over adjectives; a smart co-pilot, never a salesman. Emotional goal: replace exam anxiety with earned confidence. German-market trust cues matter (DSGVO, accuracy, no overpromising - marketing claims were deliberately stripped until features earn them).

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-SaaS landing pages: gradient text heroes, glassmorphism cards, hero metric rows, buzzword copy ("seamless", "supercharge"), uppercase tracked eyebrows on every section.
- Dated German Theorie-app clutter (Theorie24-style dense menus and stock imagery).
- Founder-settled decisions that override skill defaults: body font stays Inter (a swap to Instrument Sans was tried and reverted on 2026-08-20 by explicit founder choice; do not relitigate). Palette is strictly flat blue (#2563eb family) + emerald + neutrals, with red/amber reserved for semantic states only. No em or en dashes anywhere in user-facing copy.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence over decoration: every visual element earns its place by informing (readiness score, mistake log, progress), never by ornamenting.
2. The tool disappears into the task: in-app UI favors familiarity and density (Linear/Stripe-grade restraint) over novelty; delight is reserved for earned moments (lesson completed, readiness milestone).
3. Honest by design: never imply a capability the product does not ship; copy states facts with numbers.
4. One-hand, on-the-go: primary flows work on a 390px phone with a thumb, including in a mounted-phone driving context.
5. Bilingual parity: DE and EN receive equal typographic and layout care; German compound words must never break layouts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: body contrast >= 4.5:1 (including placeholders), keyboard navigability, 44x44px touch targets, visible focus states, alt text on informative images. Dark mode is a first-class theme inside the app. Non-native German speakers are a core audience: plain language, no idioms in critical flows.
