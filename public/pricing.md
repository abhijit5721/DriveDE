# Pricing: DriveDE

Last updated: 2026-10-16. Prices in EUR including VAT. All Pro passes are one-time payments. There is no subscription and nothing renews.

## Free

- Price: 0 EUR
- No account needed to start; the app opens as a guest.
- Included: right-before-left trainer and roundabout trainer on the landing page, the first exam situations of the Prüfungssimulation, the exam situation explanations at /pruefung/ and /exam/, lesson recording basics.
- A 7-day Pro trial starts automatically on first use (one per account and per device).

## Pro 30 days

- Price: 9,99 EUR one-time
- Access: 30 days from purchase
- Included: all exam situations with on-device grading and detailed AI feedback, the learner's own Prüfprotokoll by competence area, all manoeuvre guides, GPS lesson recording with mistake log, history and cost overview, vehicle technology questions.

## Pro 90 days

- Price: 19,99 EUR one-time
- Access: 90 days from purchase
- Included: everything in Pro 30 days. The usual choice for the three months before the test.

## Pro lifetime

- Price: 29,99 EUR one-time
- Access: no time limit
- Included: everything in Pro.

## Notes

- Payment via Stripe (card, Apple Pay, Google Pay). No app store purchase: DriveDE is a web app that can be installed on the home screen.
- Languages: German and English at every tier.
- Driving schools and instructors: discount codes for a school's learners are planned; write to hello@drivede.app.
- Refunds: within 14 days per German consumer law, see https://www.drivede.app/ (Impressum and AGB in the footer).
